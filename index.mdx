## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Call `POST /api/login` with your credentials to receive a Bearer token. Pass this token in the `Authorization` header on every subsequent request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard a Customer">
    Create a customer profile with `POST /api/save-customer`, verify their identity documents, and attach their bank account details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a Loan Application">
    Create a loan with `POST /api/save-loan`, upload required checklist documents, run credit assessment, and generate the eligibility report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All API calls require a valid Bearer token. See [Authentication](/authentication) for details on obtaining and refreshing tokens.
</Note>
